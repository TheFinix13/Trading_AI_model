## Instrument-fit notes (who devours which chart — hypotheses to test, not claims)

- **USDJPY / USDCHF (Tier 1, AL survey running):** USD-base semantics
  = Barou's home grammar; JPY session character also makes it the
  first test bed for a session-anchored Chigiri v1.1.
- **Crosses (EURGBP/EURJPY/GBPJPY…):** GBPJPY/EURJPY are high-ATR
  trend fields — Bachira's n-machine gets informative fast; EURGBP is
  a low-vol ranger — zones/fade grammar (Isagi/Rin-family) fits
  better than breakout.
- **XAUUSD (gold):** the most news-driven chart in the universe —
  natural Sae/Karasu field once S1 lands; also Barou-style power
  trends. Pip semantics differ — field card first.
- **XAGUSD (silver):** gold's beta-amplified sibling; whatever
  passes on gold gets its robustness check here, not a fresh design.
- **USOIL/UKOIL:** inventory-report rhythm (Wed EIA) = event-driven;
  candidate NEW player: an inventory-event specialist (Sae-pattern,
  different calendar feed). Session gaps need explicit handling.
- **Indices (USTEC/US500/US30/DE40/UK100/JP225):** open-auction
  structure means ORB-family weapons — the natural home for a
  Chigiri v2 or a NEW session player; also strong overnight-drift
  literature. Daily gaps = the engine's fill assumptions need a
  field-card review BEFORE any replay is believed.
- **BTCUSD:** 24/7, no sessions, fat tails — none of the current
  weapons' assumptions hold; parked until a dedicated design pass.
  Weekend bars also break the current weekday-floor monitoring.
